Standard aur express service ka complete guide

Official function ho, shaadi ka season, ya office meeting – aapka suit perfect condition mein hona chahiye. Par sawaal yeh hai ki suit dry cleaning kitne time mein hoti hai? Is blog mein hum aapko timing, process aur prices ke baare mein sab kuch batayenge.

Standard Dry Cleaning Time: 48 Ghante

Shree Ganesh Dry Cleaners mein standard dry cleaning ka time 48 ghante (2 din) hota hai. Is time mein aapke suit ko complete cycle se guzara jaata hai – inspection, stain treatment, dry cleaning, steam pressing, aur final quality check.

Express Service: 24 Ghante Me Ready

Agar aapko suit jald chahiye, toh express service bhi available hai. Ismein aapka suit sirf 24 ghante mein ready ho jaata hai. Express service par thoda extra charge lagta hai lekin quality mein koi compromise nahi hota.

Puri Process Kya Hai?

  1. Inspection: Fabric type aur stains ka check
  2. Pre-treatment: Collar, cuffs aur stains ka special treatment
  3. Dry Cleaning: Eco-friendly solvents se cleaning
  4. Steam Pressing: Creases hataana aur shape maintain karna
  5. Final Check: Quality assurance ke baad delivery
