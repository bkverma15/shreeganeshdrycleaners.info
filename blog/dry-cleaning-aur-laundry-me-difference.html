Dry Cleaning Aur Laundry Me Difference Kya Hai?

Janiye kab dry cleaning aur kab laundry service chahiye

Home › Blog › Dry Cleaning vs Laundry

Bahut log sochte hain ki dry cleaning aur laundry same hain. Par aisa nahi hai. Yeh dono alag-alag processes hain aur alag type ke kapdon ke liye use hote hain. Is blog mein hum aapko dry cleaning aur laundry ke beech ka difference simple Hindi-English mix mein samjhayenge.

Dry Cleaning Kya Hai?

Dry cleaning ek chemical-based cleaning process hai jisme paani ki jagah special solvents (like perchloroethylene) ka use kiya jaata hai. Yeh process un kapdon ke liye hai jo paani se damage ho sakte hain.

Kab dry cleaning chahiye?

  • Silk saree ya delicate fabrics
  • Wool coats aur blazers
  • Formal suits aur ties
  • Heavy stains wale kapde
  • Leather ya suede items
  • Embroidery ya zari wale kapde

Laundry Kya Hai?

Laundry water-based washing hai jisme paani, detergent aur machine ka use hota hai. Yeh aapke daily wear kapdon ki regular washing ke liye perfect hai.

Kab laundry chahiye?

  • Daily wear shirts aur t-shirts
  • Jeans aur casual pants
  • Bedsheets aur towels
  • Cotton kapde
  • Kids clothes
