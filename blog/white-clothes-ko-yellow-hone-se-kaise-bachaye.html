White Clothes Ko Yellow Hone Se Kaise Bachaye?

Safed kapdon ki original whiteness kaise maintain karein

Home › Blog › White Clothes Care Tips

Safed kapdon ki ek alag hi baat hoti hai. Par time ke saath white clothes yellow ho jaate hain, jisse unki beauty khatam ho jaati hai. Is blog mein hum aapko batayenge white clothes ko yellow hone se kaise bachaye aur unki original whiteness kaise maintain karein.

White Clothes Yellow Kyun Hote Hain?

  • Deodorants aur perfumes ka chemical reaction
  • Ganda paaniDeoaurpasse use karna
  • Storage mein nami aur dhool
  • Sunscreen aur body oils
  • Over-bleaching se fabric damage

Yellow Hone Se Bachane Ke Tips

1. Kapdon ko ulta karein

White clothes ko wash karte waqt ulta kar ke (inside out) dalein. Isse direct friction se fabric damage nahi hota aur whiteness bani rehti hai.

2. Cold water use karein

Garam paani white clothes ke fibers ko weak kar deta hai aur yellowing ko badhata hai. Hamesha cold water se wash karein.

3. Separate wash karein

White clothes ko hamesha alag dhoyein. Colors ke saath mix karne se color transfer hota hai jo whiteness ko kharab karta hai.

4. Natural bleaching

Baking soda aur vinegar ka mixture white clothes ki whiteness restore karne mein madad karta hai. Chemical bleach ka kam se kam use karein.

5. धूप mein sukayein

White clothes ko direct sunlight mein sukhane se natural bleaching hoti hai. Lekin zyada der dhool mein na chhodein, nahi toh fabric weak ho sakta hai.

6. Professional dry cleaning

Expensive white clothes (like silk, formal shirts) ke liye professional dry cleaning best option hai. Shree Ganesh Dry Cleaners mein hum eco-friendly methods use karte hain jo white clothes ki life badhate hain.
