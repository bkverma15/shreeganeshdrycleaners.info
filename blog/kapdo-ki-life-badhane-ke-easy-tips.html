Kapdo Ki Life Badhane Ke Easy Tips

10 simple tips jo aapke kapdon ko lamba time naya rakhein

Home › Blog › Kapdo ki Life Tips

Apne favourite kapde jaldi kharab ho jaate hain? Is blog mein hum aapko kapdo ki life badhane ke easy tips batayenge jo aapke kapdon ko lamba time naya aur fresh rakheinge.

1. Washing Instructions Padhein

Har garment ke andar ek care label hota hai. Us par likhi instructions follow karein. Kuch kapde cold wash require karte hain, kuch warm. Galat temperature se fabric damage hota hai.

2. Zyada Baar Na Dhoyein

Har kapda pehenne ke baad dhona zaroori nahi. Jeans, jackets, sweaters – inhe 3-4 baar pehenne ke baad hi dhona chahiye. Zyada washing se fabric fibers weak ho jaate hain.

3. Colors Ko Alag Karein

White, light aur dark colors ko alag-alag dhoyein. Isse color transfer nahi hota aur kapde apna original color maintain karte hain.

4. Cold Water Use Karein

Cold water washing se fabric fibers ko damage nahi hota aur colors bhi fade nahi hote. Garam paani sirf tough stains ke liye use karein.

5. Dry Cleaning Preferred Hai

Silk, wool, formal suits aur delicate fabrics ke liye dry cleaning best option hai. Shree Ganesh Dry Cleaners ki professional dry cleaning aapke kapdon ki life badhati hai.

6. Direct Sunlight Se Bachayein

Kapdon ko direct sunlight mein na sukhayein. Sunlight se colors fade ho jaate hain. Kapdon ko shade mein sukhayein.

7. Proper Folding & Hanging

Sweaters aur knitwear ko fold karke rakhna chahiye, hang karne se yeh stretch ho jaate hain. Suits aur shirts ko hamesha hanger par rakhein.

8. Storage Dhyaan Rakhein

Kapdon ko cool, dry place mein store karein. Nami se mold aur fungus lag sakta hai. Mothballs ya lavender packets use karein insects se bachane ke liye.

9. Stains Ko Turant Treat Karein

Koi stain lage toh turant treatment karein. Jitna late kareinge, utna stain set ho jayega aur hatana mushkil hoga.

10. Professional Care Lein
