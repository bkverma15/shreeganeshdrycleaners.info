Dry Cleaning & Laundry FAQs

Yeh woh sawaal hain jo hamare customers aksar poochte hain. Agar aapka sawaal yahan nahi hai toh contact page se poochh sakte hain.

1. Kya aap home pickup aur delivery karte hain?

Haan, bilkul! Hum Kota mein free home pickup aur delivery service provide karte hain. Aapko sirf 9950456693 par call karna hai ya WhatsApp karna hai. Hum aapke ghar se kapde collect kar lenge aur safai ke baad wapas deliver kar denge.

2. Dry cleaning mein kitna time lagta hai?

Standard dry cleaning ka time 48 ghante hai. Agar aapko jald chahiye toh express service bhi available hai jisme 24 ghante mein kapde mil jaate hain. Express service par thoda extra lagta hai.

3. Aapki service ka price kya hai?

Hamari prices bahut affordable hain: Dry Cleaning ₹70 se, Laundry ₹20/piece, Saree Cleaning ₹120 se, Coat Cleaning ₹120 se, Shoe Cleaning ₹50/pair, Carpet Cleaning ₹150 se. Pura price list yahan dekhein.

4. Kya aap delicate fabrics (silk, saree, wool) clean karte hain?

Haan, humare pass 10+ saalon ka experience hai delicate fabrics ki safai mein. Hum eco-friendly solvents use karte hain jo fabric ke fibers ko nuksaan nahi pahunchate. Silk, cotton, wool, synthetic – sab ki safai ka humein sahi tareeqa pata hai.

5. Kya aap Kota ke bahar bhi service dete hain?

Hum currently Kota city mein service provide karte hain, khas tor par Keshavpura, Sector 4, aur aas-paas ke areas mein. Agar aapke area mein service chahiye toh ek baar call karke confirm kar lijiye.

6. Aap konse chemicals use karte hain? Kya woh safe hain?

Hum eco-friendly aur skin-safe chemicals ka use karte hain. Yeh chemicals environment ko nuksan nahi pahunchate aur aapki skin ke kapdon ki quality bhi maintain rakhte hain. Hamare saare products professional grade hain.

7. Kya aap stain removal karte hain?

Haan, hum professional stain removal service provide karte hain. Chahe woh ink stain ho, oil stain, ya koi bhi tough stain – humare experts special techniques se stains hataate hain bina fabric ko damage kiye.

8. Kya aap bulk order (hotels, offices) ke liye discount dete hain?

Haan, bilkul! Bulk orders aur regular business customers ke liye special discounts available hain. Call karein ya WhatsApp karein aur hum aapko best price denge.

9. Kya aap leather jacket clean karte hain?

Haan, hum leather jackets aur leather products ki bhi cleaning karte hain, lekin iske liye special treatment chahiye hoti hai. Ek baar call karke puchh lijiye aur hum aapko sahi guidance denge.

10. Kya aap same-day delivery karte hain?

Haan, express service mein hum 24 ghante mein delivery karte hain. Same-day delivery limited items ke liye possible hai. Call karke confirm kar lijiye.
